Dongfeng Shine

Made for this exact car.

Every window, vent and switch was tested by hand on the real car. Not a generic app: it works with this car's own controls.

Talk to your car, in your language

Shine understands your voice inside the car, without an internet connection. Nothing you say is recorded or sent anywhere.

🎙️

Hands-free

Say “Hey Shine”, or press the voice button on the steering wheel, then say what you need.

🌡️

Climate

AC on or off, an exact temperature, warmer or cooler, fan speed, defrost and recirculation.

🪟

Windows and sunroof

Open, close or tilt, without reaching for a switch. Anything that moves glass is checked first.

🎵

Music and calls

Play, pause, next song, volume, or call a contact by name. Your hands stay on the wheel.

🗣️

English or Egyptian Arabic

Two versions of the app: choose the language you speak in the car. Each answers in a natural voice.

✨

Your own commands

Teach Shine your own phrases, like “winter mode” to warm up the cabin or “call home”.

⬇ تحميل النسخة العربية
  1. Download the app on your phone or computer and copy it to a USB stick (or download it on the car's screen, if it has a browser).
  2. On the car's screen, open the file and tap Install. If asked, allow installing apps from this source.
  3. Open Shine Voice once, allow the microphone and the on-screen card, then tap “Start Shine”.
  4. That's it. Say “Hey Shine” any time, or press the steering-wheel voice button. To open the menu later (settings, updates, a report to support), tap the Shine icon or say “open settings”.
⚠️ Made and tested on the Dongfeng Shine. A different model year or trim may have a different system: if Shine can't control your car, open its menu and press “Send report”, so support for your car can be added.

Updates arrive by themselves once the app is installed. Questions or a report code: support@shinevoiceofficial.com. Privacy policy
