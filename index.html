Get Shine Voice

Free, sideloaded install — no Play Store needed.

⬇ Download APK
  1. Download the APK to your phone, then copy it to a USB stick (or download directly on the head unit if it has a browser).
  2. On the head unit, open the APK file and tap Install.
  3. Open Shine Voice once, allow the microphone and on-screen card, then tap “Start the assistant.”
  4. That's it — the icon hides itself. Say “Hey Shine” any time, or press the steering-wheel voice button.
⚠️ Built and tested on a Dongfeng Shine (Qinggan/PATEO head unit, Android 8.1). Other trims — including the GS — and other vehicles or head units have not been tested and may wire the CAN bus differently.

Shine Voice — an independent, unofficial project. Not affiliated with Dongfeng Motor or Qinggan.
